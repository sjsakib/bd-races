 About the Event
Our Earth is under threat from pollution, deforestation, and climate change. Run For The Earth 2026 is not just a race—it’s a movement for environmental sustainability and a call to action for a greener planet.
Join us on Friday, 18 December 2026, at Hatirjheel, Dhaka, and let your footsteps speak louder than words.

 Why Run For The Earth?
