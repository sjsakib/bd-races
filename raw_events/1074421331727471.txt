TFS 10K - an experience beyond the run.
The Fitness Squad began its journey with a simple goal, to build a healthier and more active community. What started with small steps has now grown into one of the fastest rising fitness communities in the country, especially across Uttara, Tongi and nearby areas. TFS 10K is not just a race, it’s a festive celebration of stamina, strength, culture and community spirit.
