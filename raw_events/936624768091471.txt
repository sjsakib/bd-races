Welcome to The Oldest AIMS Race in Bangladesh!
JOLSHIRI RUNBANGLA HALF MARATHON 2025 is an annual RUNBANGLA race organized with the goal to inspire people and engage them more in the domain of physical fitness.
The race will be a continuation of a wonderful Half Marathon at a picturesque location. The beautiful Jolshiri Abashon area, Rupganj ,a 30-minute drive from Gulshan 2 circle ,is a housing society for military officers and is managed by the Bangladesh Army. The road communication to the venue is already good and will further improve by the time our race takes place.
Your running track will consist of a well-connected road network within the gated community, surrounded by greenery and lakes. This race is a joint collaboration between Jolshiri Abashon Authority and RUNBANGLA.
At RUNBANGLA, we always strive to improve, reorganize, and deliver a better experience for runners.
The race will have three main categories, each with 1,000 slots.
