 Something beautiful is coming to Rajshahi… 
For years, we watched major running events happen in different cities.
This time, North Bengal will have its own story.
On the winter morning of November 20, 2026, the roads of Rajshahi will witness something special for the very first time.
 The beauty of Padma

 The calmness of the Green City

 The youthful spirit of University of Rajshahi

 The culture, warmth, and pride of North Bengal
— all together in one unforgettable running celebration.
 Rajshahi Half Marathon 2026

 20 November 2026

 5:45 AM
Categories:

 21.1 KM (Half Marathon)

 10 KM (Challenge Run)

 5 KM (Health Run)
This will not be just about timing or competition.
This event is being planned as a celebration of life, health, friendship, community, and the unique vibe of Rajshahi.
Whether you are a professional runner, beginner, student, traveler, or someone who simply loves the energy of people coming together — this event is for you.
We want every participant to feel:

 Welcome

 Inspired

 Proud to be part of something different
Expect a true Rajshahi City experience:

 Padma riverside vibe

 Beautiful winter morning atmosphere

 Green & clean city theme

 Cultural identity of Rajshahi

 Fun, energy, music, memories & togetherness
And yes…
Everyone will leave with a smile. 
Registration details, route map, jersey, medal reveal, and many exciting surprises will be announced soon.
