The 4th edition of the Chunati.com Marathon is set to take place on 26th December along the iconic Ishak Mia Road in Chunati, Lohagara, Chattogram. With its rich heritage and natural beauty, Chunati offers runners a one-of-a-kind experience through scenic hills and vibrant culture.
We are thrilled to officially open registration for the Chunati.com Marathon 2025. Get ready to run where history meets nature!
