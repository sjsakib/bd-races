2nd Endurance Camp 2026 – Alutila, Khagrachhari
Gear up for the ultimate test of endurance, grit, and adventure!
If you are ready to elevate your running game, conquer wild trails, and train under the guidance of Bangladesh’s top certified coaches and mentors, this is where you need to be. We are heading completely off-grid into the breathtaking, rugged terrains of Khagrachhari for two days of serious-level training.
 Event Overview
* Dates: 28th – 29th August, 2026
* Venue: Alutila, Khagrachhari (Accommodation above 1,000 feet, fully detached from city life)
* Total Distance: 30 km
* Total Elevation Gain: 1,000 meters
* Food: Authentic local cuisine to fuel your body
 Training & Adventure Itinerary
# Day 1: Conquering the West
* AM | Arrival & Briefing: Early morning check-in followed by a hearty breakfast (7:00 AM - 9:00 AM). We kick off with an official Camp Briefing to set expectations and strategies.
* PM | Alutila West Run: Hit the trails for an intense run covering the scenic yet challenging Risang Falls and Germplasm Trails.
#Day 2: The East Matrix & Technical Trailing
* AM | Alutila East Run: A high-endurance morning run conquering Mayung Kopal, the mysterious Alutila Cave, and a thrilling Chengi River Crossing.
* PM | De-brief & Expert Consultation: Camp recap followed by exclusive 1:1 consultations with top mentors and coaches
 Exclusive 1:1 Mentorship & Expert Guidance
This isn't just a run; it’s an elite learning experience. You will get direct access to Bangladesh's top endurance experts to master:
* Race Prep & Strategy: How to pace and plan your next big milestone.
* Gadgets & Gear: Optimizing your watches, shoes, and trail gear.
* Fueling & Hydration Plan: Personalized nutrition strategies for long-distance endurance.
* Injury Prevention: Keeping your body resilient under extreme stress.
* Crisis Management: How to mentally and physically handle unwanted situations on the trail.
 Note: Spaces for this elite endurance camp are strictly limited to ensure high-quality 1:1 coaching. Prepare your mind and body for a serious level of training.
