 Important Notes on Prize Money:
- Prize money is awarded to podium finishers only if a minimum of 20 participants are registered in that gender category.
- If 10–19 participants: Prize money for Champion & 1st Runner Up only.
- Below 10 participants: Champion only.
- Podium is determined by Gun Time.
- Winners must be present at the awards ceremony. Unclaimed prizes will be forfeited.
- Podium positions are for Amateur Athletes only.


 IMPORTANT RULES & GUIDELINES
General Rules:
— Participants must meet the minimum age requirement for their chosen category.
— All registrations are online only. No on-site registrations will be accepted on race day.
— Valid photo ID is required for race kit collection at the Race Expo.
— All race kits must be collected during the Race Expo. Kits will not be distributed on race day.
— All participants must agree to the event waiver during registration.
Race Day Rules:
— Timing chips are mandatory for Half Marathon, Challenge Run & FNF Run participants.
— Race bibs must be worn visibly at all times. No bib = disqualification.
— Participants must begin at their designated start times.
— All runners must stay on the official race route and follow race officials and volunteers.
— Littering is strictly prohibited. Please use designated bins.
— Pets and strollers are not permitted on the racecourse.
— Medical assistance is available along the course. Seek help immediately if needed.
Cancellation & Refund Policy:
— The event proceeds rain or shine. Severe weather may delay the start — no refunds will be issued.
— Registrations are transferable to another participant with a transfer fee.
— No refunds under any circumstances, including injury or illness.
Conduct & Fair Play:
— All participants must show respect toward fellow runners, volunteers, and event staff.
— Any disputes must be reported to race officials immediately after the race.
