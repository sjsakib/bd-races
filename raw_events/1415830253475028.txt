Tiger pace Half marathon 2026 season 1
Organized by: H2O event Management & co-organized by: FANDT Events
Date: January 23, 2025
Venue: Hatirjheel Circuit, Dhaka
Theme: Push the Pace, Lift the Community

 Race Categories
Half Marathon – 21.1 KM
Fun Run – 7.5 KM
Kids’ Run – 2 KM (approx.)
 Early Bird Registration Fees
21.1 KM → 1299
7.5 KM → 1299
2 KM (Kids) → 749
 Regular Registration Fees
21.1 KM → 1499
7.5 KM → 1399
2 KM (Kids) → 999
 Cut-Off Times
21.1 KM → 3 hours 30 minutes
7.5 KM → 1 hour 10 minutes
2 KM Kids’ Run → No cut off time
(Runners finishing after the cut-off will not be eligible for official timing or finisher medals.)
 Prize Money: (will be announced soon)
21.1 KM → Top three finishers of each category (Both male, female and veteran runners) will get crests and prize money (At least 10 participants are necessary for podium eligibility in each category).
7.5 KM → Top three finishers of each category (Both male, female and veteran runners) will get crests and prize money (At least 7 participants are necessary for podium eligibility in each category).
2 KM (Kids) → Top three finishers (Both male, female) will get crests and gifts for all.
 Racer Benefits

 Official Event Jersey

 Race Bib & Timing Chip (21.1K & 7.5K)

 Finisher Medal

 Certificate

 Multiple Hydration Stations During the Race – To keep runners hydrated during the race, hydration stations will be positioned thoughtfully around the course. These stations will include water, glucose, saline, dates, lemon, ginger, and many more options. These stations will be well marked and within easy reach

 Washroom Facilities

 Post-Race Snacks – All competitors will get free post-race food when they reach the finish line. These snacks are meant to help you celebrate your success and refuel.

 Ambulance, Medical & First Aid Support along the route

 Event Photography & Media Coverage
 Terms & Conditions

2. Registration is non-refundable and non-transferable.
3. Participants must wear the official bib number visibly during the run.
4. Age requirements:
21.1 KM: Anyone (Above 16 years)
7.5 KM: Anyone (Above 14 years)
2 KM Kids’ Run: 4–13 years (guardian consent required)
5. Organizers reserve the right to modify the route or schedule due to safety, weather, or other conditions.
6. Unsportsmanlike conduct will lead to disqualification.
7. All participants must comply with Race Crews' instructions.
