Run Where the Sun Meets the Sea
Date: 6:00 AM, Saturday, 17 October 2026
Location: Kuakata Sea Beach, Patuakhali, Bangladesh
Organized by: The Athlete X
About the Event
Experience one of Bangladesh’s most scenic running events at the Kuakata Half Marathon 2026. Known as the “Daughter of the Sea,” Kuakata is the only place in Bangladesh where you can witness both the sunrise and sunset over the Bay of Bengal. This event combines endurance, tourism, and the breathtaking beauty of the southern coastline, inviting runners from across Bangladesh and beyond to challenge themselves on an unforgettable course.
Whether you are an experienced runner or taking on your first race, Kuakata Half Marathon 2026 offers an inspiring journey through coastal roads, sea breeze, and spectacular natural landscapes.
