 “চট্টলার হাওয়া, সাগরের ধারা — দৌড়াই চট্টগ্রাম ধুমাই যামু রে!” 
Get ready, Bangladesh! The spirit of the port city is rising — P2P ActivePulse Chattogram Marathon 2025 is here to set the coast alive! Happening on December 13, 2025 (Saturday) at the breathtaking Patenga Sea Beach, this grand celebration isn’t just a marathon — it’s a festival of passion, pride, and purpose. With the roar of the waves and the rhythm of your heartbeat, Chattogram is calling you to run for glory, unity, and the spirit of our beautiful Bangladesh! 
Date: December 13, 2025 (Saturday)
Location: Patenga Sea Beach (Walkway), Chattogram
Distances for Everyone:
42.195 KM Marathon
21.095 KM Half Marathon
10.55 KM Challenge Run
1 KM Fun Run
Whether you're a seasoned runner, a first-timer, or simply looking to enjoy a memorable day out—this is YOUR race!
 Together, We Move Toward

 SDG 3 – Good Health & Well-Being

 SDG 6 – Clean Water & Sanitation

 SDG 11 – Sustainable Cities & Communities
The ActivePulse Chattogram Marathon stands as a symbol of health, unity, and sustainability — promoting an active lifestyle while connecting communities across Bangladesh. Feel the rhythm, the unity, the adrenaline — let’s make history TOGETHER!

 Race Categories & Minimum Age Requirements
Full Marathon (42.195 KM): 18 years & above
Half Marathon (21.095 KM): 16 years & above
Challenge Run (10.55 KM): 14 years & above
Fun Run (1 KM): No age limit — open to all (perfect for kids, families & beginners)
 General Rules & Guidelines
Age Limits: Participants must meet the minimum age requirement for their chosen category.
Registration: All participants must register online within the registration period. No on-site registration will be accepted on race day.
ID Verification: A valid photo ID is required for race kit collection at the Race Expo.
Race Kit Pickup: All kits must be collected during the Race Expo. No distribution will be done on race day or via mail.
Waiver Agreement: All runners must agree to the event waiver during online registration.
 Race Day Rules
Timing Chips: Mandatory for Full Marathon, Half Marathon, and Challenge Run participants to ensure official timing.
Bib Numbers: Bibs must be worn visibly at all times during the race. Running without a visible bib may lead to disqualification.
Start Times: All participants must start at their designated flag-off times.
Course Compliance: Runners must stay on the official route and follow guidance from race marshals and volunteers.
Littering: Strictly prohibited — please dispose of waste at designated bins. Let’s keep Chattogram clean! 

Medical Support: Medical aid and hydration stations will be available throughout the course. Seek assistance from volunteers or medics if needed.
Pets & Strollers: Not allowed on the racecourse for safety reasons.
 Prize Money Rules
Eligibility: Prize money will only be awarded if a category meets the minimum required number of participants.
Winners: Prizes will be awarded to the top 3 male and female finishers in the Full Marathon, Half Marathon, and Challenge Run.
Presence Required: Winners must be present at the official award ceremony to claim their prizes — no claims afterward.
Verification: Identity and age verification may be required prior to awarding prizes.
 Cancellation & Changes
Weather Policy: The event will take place rain or shine. In case of extreme conditions, the start may be delayed, but no refunds will be issued.
Transfer Policy: Registrations may be transferred to another runner within the given period (with applicable fees).
Refund Policy: No refunds under any circumstances, including injury, illness, or travel issues.
 Conduct & Fair Play
Sportsmanship: Respect fellow runners, event staff, and volunteers at all times. Unsporting behavior may result in disqualification.
Complaints: Any dispute or concern must be reported to race officials immediately after the race.
Spirit of Running: Run fair, run strong, and uphold the ActivePulse spirit — Fitness, Friendship & Fun! 
 Group Registration Discount
