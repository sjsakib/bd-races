TURAAG ACTIVE DHAKA 25K 2027 | 4th Edition : ROAD TO GLORY
Run Bangladesh is incredibly proud to announce the 4th edition of our signature premium event! Mark your calendars for Friday, 29 January 2027, as we bring the ultimate endurance challenge back to the vibrant streets of Dhaka.
As the pioneer of the 25K distance in the country, this is more than just a race—it is a celebration of human endurance, community, and the relentless pursuit of greatness. Whether you are aiming for a personal best, stepping up to a new distance, or simply running for the joy of it, this is your moment to shine on an international-standard platform.
 ABOUT RUN BANGLADESH & THE DHAKA 25K JOURNEY**
Run Bangladesh was founded with a clear vision: to elevate local races to international standards and unite running enthusiasts from Bangladesh and around the world. As the driving force behind the Turaag Active Dhaka 25K, we have proudly pioneered a new era of endurance sports in the country.
The journey began with our inaugural edition on March 8, 2024, an event that brought together 2,000 runners from 15 different countries. This historic first season introduced the 25K distance to Dhaka and laid a foundation built on energy, discipline, and community spirit.
Driven by the motto "Run for Better Bangladesh", we have continuously raised the bar. From introducing cutting-edge RFID timing to securing prestigious AIMS membership and World Athletics course certifications, every milestone reflects our commitment to excellence. Our mission goes beyond organising a race; it is about showcasing Dhaka's beauty to a global audience and fostering a healthier, more active lifestyle for all citizens. Now, entering our 4th Edition, the Dhaka 25K stands proudly as the nation's signature road race.
RACE CATEGORIES
Choose your challenge and join thousands of passionate runners:
*25K (The Signature Run): The ultimate test of endurance for seasoned runners looking to conquer our flagship distance.
*10K Run: A fast, thrilling distance perfect for intermediate runners and those looking to set a new personal record.
* 3K Kids/Family / Wheelchair Run: A fantastic way to get the whole family moving and inspire the next generation of runners!
OUR LEGACY: HIGHLIGHTS FROM THE LAST 3 EDITIONS
Since its inception, the Dhaka 25K has reshaped the running landscape in Bangladesh. Here is what we have achieved together over the past three record-breaking editions:
* A Pioneering Distance: We successfully introduced and popularized the 25K race format in Bangladesh, giving local athletes a new milestone to conquer.
*Global Standards: From day one, we have maintained a premium racing experience with a beautifully mapped route that holds official AIMS and World Athletics certifications.
* International Appeal: Over the last three editions, we have hosted thousands of runners, including a growing roster of international athletes and elite competitors, putting Dhaka on the global running map.
* Flawless Execution: Consistently delivering premium race expo, and unparalleled on-course medical, hydration support, race track management.
WHY YOU CAN'T MISS THE 4TH EDITION**
We are taking everything you loved about the previous years and elevating it! Expect a bigger race village, electrifying cheering zones, exclusive finisher medals.
Prepare to push your limits, break your barriers, and join the RBAN TEAM on the Road to Glory!
