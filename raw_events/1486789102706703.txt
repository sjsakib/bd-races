RUN FOR INTEGRITY 2026
Say NO to Corruption
 Date: Saturday, 12 December 2026

 Venue: Hatirjheel, Dhaka

 Race Categories: 7.5KM , 15KM & 21.1KM
Integrity is not just a value; it is the foundation of a fair, transparent, and sustainable society.
Join us at Run for Integrity 2026 (SAY NO TO CORRUPTION), a unique movement that brings together corporate leaders, policymakers, development partners, youth, athletes, students, and citizens to stand united against corruption and promote a culture of integrity in Bangladesh.
Organized by UN Global Compact Network Bangladesh (GCNB) in collaboration with Maritime Anti-Corruption Network (MACN) and Race coordinated by Run Globally, this landmark event aims to transform awareness into action by inspiring individuals and organizations to champion ethical practices, transparency, and responsible leadership.
Why Join?

 Promote integrity and transparency in society

 Support ethical business practices and good governance

 Connect with changemakers from diverse sectors

 Run alongside corporate leaders, youth, and professional athletes

 Be part of a nationwide movement for positive change

 Be a part of AIMS Certified running event.
