Dhaka Move – 2nd Edition
Register is now open... register here https://register.runbangladesh.com/dhaka-move-26/
Ready for the ultimate autumn getaway right in the heart of the city? Dhaka Move – 2nd Edition is here! With the heavy rains behind us, early October is the perfect time to grab your friends, gather the family, and hit the beautiful streets of Hatirjheel for a massive, unforgettable fun run.
Date: October 2, 2026
Location: Hatirjheel
Distances: 4.6 Miles | 9.3 Miles
Track Type: Anti-clockwise
Registration Opening Soon!
Given these warm conditions, we are placing a massive emphasis on course safety. You bring the stamina, and we will make sure you have all the hydration and medical support you need to cross the finish line safely!
For our 2nd Edition, we are stripping away the unnecessary extras and putting all the focus exactly where it belongs: on the run and the community.
To keep this event accessible and affordable for runners of all levels, there are no heavy kit bags and no mandatory event gifts. Instead, you get a premium, fully supported race experience for a fraction of the usual cost.

Whether you are tackling the 4.6-mile sprint or pushing your limits on the 9.3-mile route, this event is open to everyone. Lace up, embrace the autumn heat, and let's move Dhaka together! See less
