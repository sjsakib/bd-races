Moglabazar 10K Run 2026
Keep Running Keep Growing
To promote health awareness, fitness, and an active lifestyle in Moglabazar, Moglabazar Runners Community is proudly organizing the Moglabazar 10K Run for the very first time.
This is not just a race—
It is a commitment to moving forward together towards a healthier life, the courage to push beyond our limits, and an initiative to build a more active and vibrant community.
Get ready for an unforgettable running experience!
