Run for the Ocean. Run for the Future.
Experience one of Bangladesh’s most scenic road races at the Bay of Bengal Marathon 2026, taking place on 26 September 2026 at the breathtaking Inani Beach, Cox’s Bazar.
This is more than just a marathon—it’s a movement dedicated to raising awareness about protecting the precious marine ecosystem of the Bay of Bengal. Every step you take will symbolize our shared commitment to preserving ocean life for future generations.

Why Join?
* Run along the spectacular coastline of Inani Beach.
* Experience one of the most beautiful marathon routes in Bangladesh.
* Be part of a campaign promoting ocean conservation and environmental sustainability.
* Challenge yourself while supporting a meaningful cause.
Whether you’re an elite athlete, a passionate runner, or someone who believes in protecting nature, the Bay of Bengal Marathon 2026 welcomes you to become part of this unforgettable journey.
