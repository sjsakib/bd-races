ActivePulse Women’s Run : Season 3 : Where Her Strength Becomes Her Story
Get ready — ActivePulse Women’s Run : Season 3 is here! A movement returns. A celebration grows stronger. A new chapter begins. Bigger, bolder, and more inspiring than ever, we are all set to host a remarkable celebration dedicated to the strength, confidence, resilience, and unstoppable spirit of women.
Date: 15 January 2027
Venue: Hatirjheel Amphitheatre
Step into an experience created to celebrate every woman’s journey. This year’s Women’s Run is more than just a race — it is a platform where stories are shared, confidence is built, and strength is celebrated. Whether you are taking your first step into running, chasing a personal milestone, or simply joining to celebrate movement and togetherness — this event is your space to shine. Feel the energy of thousands of women coming together with purpose, passion, and pride. Every stride represents courage, every finish line reflects achievement, and every story reminds us of the incredible strength within every woman.
Expect a morning filled with positivity, inspiration, sisterhood, and movement — a celebration where women from all walks of life come together to motivate, empower, and inspire one another. Come be part of a movement that celebrates her journey, her strength, and her story. Because this year, it’s about discovering the power within and turning every step into a story.
“Where Her Strength Becomes Her Story.”
Race Categories
There will be two race distances available:
Challenge Run — 7.321 KM (For women who are ready to challenge themselves, push their limits, and celebrate their running journey)
Fun Run — 2 KM (A joyful and relaxed run designed for beginners, families, and anyone who wants to experience the happiness of running.)
Time Limit
No time limit for any category — run at your own pace, because every step matters.
Every finisher will be celebrated and crowned with a Finisher Medal.
Participant Perks & Race Amenities
- Finisher Flower Crown (For all queens crossing the finish line)
- Premium Finisher Medal (Foreign-made)
- Official Event T-Shirt
- Personalized Race Bib (Name printed on bib)
- Chip Timing System (For accurate competitive results)
- Hydration Stations along the race route
- Post-Race Refreshments
- Cash Prizes for Challenge Run winners (Details to be announced)
- Professional Race Photography
- Access to Race Expo for kit collection and pre-race experience
- Washroom Facilities near start/finish area
- First Aid & Medical Support by official health partners
- E-Certificate after successful participation
- Dedicated Photo Booths
- Pre-Race Warm-Up Session conducted by professional trainers

Prize Money Eligibility Rules
- Prize money for Champion, 1st Runner-Up, and 2nd Runner-Up will only be awarded if there are at least 20 participants in the category.
- Prize money for Champion and 1st Runner-Up will only be awarded if there are at least 10 participants in the category.
- If fewer than 10 participants participate, only the Champion will receive prize money.
General Rules & Guidelines
Age Limits: Participants must meet the minimum age requirement for their selected category.
Registration: All participants must register online within the registration period. No on-site registration will be available on race day.
ID Verification: A valid photo ID is required during Race Kit Collection.
Race Kit Pickup: All kits must be collected during the Race Expo. No race kit distribution will take place on race day or through courier.
Waiver Agreement: All participants must accept the event waiver during registration.
Race Day Rules
- Timing chips are mandatory for Challenge Run participants.
- Bib numbers must remain visible throughout the race.
- Participants must start at their designated flag-off time.
- Runners must follow the official route and instructions from race marshals.
- Littering is strictly prohibited.
- Medical support and hydration stations will be available.
- Pets and strollers are not allowed on the race route.
Cancellation & Changes
Weather Policy: The event will take place rain or shine. In case of extreme weather conditions, the schedule may be adjusted. No refunds will be issued.
Transfer Policy: Registrations are non-transferable.
Refund Policy: No refunds will be provided under any circumstances, including injury, illness, or travel issues.
Conduct & Fair Play
Sportsmanship: Respect fellow runners, volunteers, and event officials at all times.
Complaints: Any concerns must be reported to race officials immediately after the race.
Spirit of Running: Run fair. Run strong. Celebrate every journey.
