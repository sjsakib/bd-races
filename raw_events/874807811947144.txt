Lace Up for Season 3: BM LP GAS RRC Half Marathon 2026! 

The wait is over, running community! Road Runners Chattogram (RRC) is thrilled to announce the return of the BM LP GAS RRC Half Marathon for its spectacular third season. Mark your calendars for October 23, 2026, as we take to the streets of Chattogram to push our limits and celebrate fitness!
Whether you are a seasoned marathoner or a beginner taking your first steps into the world of running, we have a distance for you:

 Race Categories:
21.1K Half Marathon: The ultimate test of endurance.
10K Run: The perfect challenge for speed and stamina.
Event Details:

 Date: October 23, 2026

 Location: Patenga Sea Beach , Chattogram
Organized by: Road Runners Chattogram (RRC)
Join hundreds of runners from across the country in this high-energy event. Experience the thrill of the race, the cheers of the crowd, and the glory of the finish line!
Stay tuned to our page. Let’s make Season 3 the biggest one yet!
