Run For Life: Hope in Every Step 
Every step we take has the power to break the silence. Every kilometer we run can spark a ray of hope.
In honor of World Suicide Prevention Day (10th September), the Bangladesh Association of Psychiatrists (BAP) is proud to present "Run For Life" on 12th September. Let’s lace up our running shoes, come together as a community, and run to remind everyone that their lives matter.
 WARNING: SLOTS ARE EXTREMELY LIMITED!
To ensure a premium and safe experience for everyone, we are capping the event at strictly 1,000 runners total across both distances. Set your reminders—registration opens soon and slots will sell out fast!
 Event Details
*Event Date: 12 September
* Reporting Time: 5:00 AM
* Location: Hatirjheel, Dhaka
* Registration Opens: 3rd August
 Race Categories & Fees
Choose your challenge and run for a cause:
* 7.5K Run: 750 Taka
* 15K Run: 850 Taka
# Every Runner Receives:
Your registration fee is fully inclusive of a premium race kit:
* Customized Premium Finisher Medal
* Premium Event Jersey
* Hydration Support
* Race Day Support
 How to Register
Mark your calendar for 3rd August! Don't miss out on being one of the 1,000 runners making a difference.
Let's run to heal, run to support, and run for life. See you at the starting line!
