Rangamati Hill Half Marathon 2026
Run with Nature, Run with Lake
Where hills rise, lakes shimmer, and running meets culture — welcome to one of the most scenic races in Bangladesh.
Organized by The Athlete X, the Rangamati Hill Half Marathon 2026 invites runners to experience a breathtaking hill track surrounded by nature, indigenous communities, and vibrant local culture.
This run is more than a race. It’s a journey through the heart of Rangamati — just days before Biju, one of the most colorful and celebrated festivals of the hill communities, making the atmosphere even more lively and meaningful.
