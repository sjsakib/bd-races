The Moulvibazar Half Marathon 2026 is Back for Season 5!


This year's event promises to be more exciting than ever, offering three distances for participants to choose from:


1. 21.1K Half Marathon
2. 10K Distance
3. 1k Kids run


Event Details:
- 10K Distance: Open to participants aged 15 and above.
- 21.1K Half Marathon: Open to participants aged 18 and above.
- 1K Kids Run: For the 1 Kilometer Kids Run, children must be under 10 years of age.




Exciting Prizes:
Top 5 male and female finishers in both distances will receive prize money!


Registration Fees:
- 21.1K– 1,350 BDT
- 10K – 1,250 BDT
- 1K - 750 BDT


Registration Starts: 30th October 2025


Don’t miss your chance to be part of this iconic event! Stay tuned for more updates and be ready to lace up for an unforgettable race experience!
