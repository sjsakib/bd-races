Run Far. Run Smart. Run Injury-Free.

We’ve been quietly training a small group at Ramna Park for a while now. The results have been amazing — and it’s time to open the doors.
This is Ramna Training Camp.
Whether you’ve never run a single kilometer or you’re chasing your first ultra, this camp gives you one thing most runners never get: a real plan, built by a real coach, designed to keep you running for years — not weeks.
No guesswork. No copy-pasted YouTube plans. Just a clear path forward, and a community that runs it with you.


WHAT’S INCLUDED

 Your own training plan — built around your goal, whether that’s 5K, 10K, half, full marathon, or ultra

 Direct coach consultancy — ask questions, get adjustments, never train blind

 Sunday coached sessions — tempo runs, intervals, long runs, drills, all at Ramna Park
 Weekday training, your way — follow your plan at your own pace, with check-ins along the way

 Video posture analysis — we record your running form and break it down to fix issues before they turn into injuries

 Injury-prevention built in — strength, mobility, and recovery, not just mileage

 A community that shows up — train next to people who’ll push you, support you, and run with you


IS THIS FOR YOU?
Yes, if you’re:

 A complete beginner who wants to start the right way

 A busy professional who needs structure that actually fits your week

 An intermediate runner stuck at the same pace for too long

 A marathoner or ultra-runner who wants real coaching, not just company
Show up with consistency. We’ll handle the rest.
