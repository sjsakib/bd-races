 Run4GoodCauses 2026 | Running for Education, Hope & Humanity
 Date: Sept 22-26, 2026

 Location: Sajek • Khagrachari • Rangamati • Bandarban • Thanchi • Alikadam
Every Step Matters. Every Kilometer Creates Change.
Run4GoodCauses is a humanitarian, non-political, and non-commercial ultra-trail running expedition dedicated to promoting peace, education, and equal opportunities for children living in the remote hill tract communities of Bangladesh.
This is not a race. It is a journey of purpose.
A team of five experienced ultra-distance runners, supported by a professional crew, will cover approximately 320 kilometers across some of the country's most challenging mountain trails, conquering 8,000+ meters of elevation gain over five consecutive days. Every step taken represents our commitment to ensuring that children in these underserved communities have access to a brighter future.
Our Mission
We believe every child deserves the opportunity to learn, grow, and dream.
Through this initiative, we aim to:
 Support access to quality education for children in remote communities.
 Help provide nutritious food, proper sanitation, and basic healthcare.
 Contribute to building and improving educational facilities.
 Raise awareness about the challenges faced by underprivileged children in the hill tracts.
 Inspire individuals, organizations, and communities to embrace social responsibility through endurance sports and humanitarian action.
Our Vision
To create a future where every child—regardless of geography, ethnicity, or economic background—has access to education, healthcare, nutrition, dignity, and equal opportunities.
At the same time, we aspire to establish endurance sports as a powerful platform for social impact, encouraging athletes and communities to combine physical endurance with compassion, leadership, and meaningful action.
Expedition Overview
 Campaign Duration: Sept 22-26, 2026

 Distance: Approximately 320 km

 Elevation Gain: 8,000+ meters

 Team: Five ultra-distance runners with dedicated support crews

 Route: Sajek → Khagrachari → Rangamati → Bandarban → Thanchi → Alikadam
Why We Run
We run because some children still walk miles just to reach a classroom.
We run because education should never depend on where a child is born.
We run because endurance means little unless it inspires hope and creates opportunities for others.
