 XVR SUMMER RUN 2025 - Special Wave
Due to overwhelming requests, XVR is excited to announce a Special Wave of 100 exclusive slots for the XVR SUMMER RUN 2025 — Registration Going on.
 Event Date: 25th June – 20th July 2025

 Location: Virtual (Run from anywhere!)
 Registration Details:
- Special Wave Registration: Open
- Registration Fee: 599 BDT

 Registration Link: https://forms.gle/ukzmhKo6xotLCetg8
 Event Details:
- Distances: 10 KM (Virtual Run);
- Recording Timeline: 25th June – 20th July 2025
- Cut-Off Time: No Cut-Off Time – Everyone Can Run! New runners, this is your moment! No pressure, just pure joy—walk, jog, or run at your own pace. Every step matters.
 Participation Rewards:
- Special Edition China Metal Medal;
- E-Certificate;
- E-BIB Number;
- Surprise Goodies;
 Submission & Assessment Criteria:
1) Confirmation & E-BIB: You’ll get a confirmation & E-BIB via Email within 24 hours.
2) Run Tracking App: Strava will be the official app to record your runs.
3) Virtual Run Period: 25th June – 20th July 2025
4) Run Completion: Total 10 KM in maximum two (2) attempts. Each attempt must be at least 5 KM.
5) Submit your run information through the form provided after registration.
6) Run Data Submission: 25th June – 20th July 2025.
 Note: Violating event guidelines may result in disqualification.
 Important Information for Participants:
- Registration is non-transferable & non-refundable.
- XVR reserves the right to change event details or disqualify participants if necessary.
- No Run, No Souvenir! You must submit your run data before the deadline to claim your earned rewards.
- 
 Note: The event authorities will not be responsible if you are unable to run due to physical illness or if you are unable to record and submit your run within the specified time.
- 
 Note: After running within the given time, The run data must be saved with event name, E-BIB number, and hashtag #XVR_XVirtualRace #XVRSummerRun.
[For Example: XVR Summer Run 2025 (BIB: 41*** ), #XVR_XVirtualRace #XVRSummerRun]
