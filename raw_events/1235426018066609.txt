"Run the Bay. Move the World."
Bangladesh, are you ready? The spirit of the port city is rising once again — the ActivePulse Chattogram Marathon 2026 is here, bigger, bolder, and more prestigious than ever!
The roar of the waves at Patenga Sea Beach and the rhythm of your heartbeat will together write a new chapter in history. This isn't just a race — it's the pride of the port city, the voice of Bangladesh, and a promise to move toward the world. 

From the Bay. For the World.

RACE CATEGORIES & AGE REQUIREMENTS
Full Marathon (42.195 KM): 18 years & above
Half Marathon (21.095 KM): 16 years & above
Challenge Run (10.55 KM): 14 years & above
Fun Run (1 KM): No age limit — open to all (perfect for kids, families & beginners)
 GENERAL RULES & GUIDELINES
Age Limits: Participants must meet the minimum age requirement for their chosen category.
Registration: Online only. No on-site registration will be accepted on race day.
ID Verification: A valid photo ID is required for kit collection at the Race Expo.
Race Kit Pickup: Expo only — no distribution on race day or via mail.
Waiver Agreement: All runners must agree to the event waiver during online registration.
 RACE DAY RULES
Timing Chips: Mandatory for Full Marathon, Half Marathon & Challenge Run.
Bib Numbers: Must be worn visibly at all times. Running without a visible bib may result in disqualification.
Start Times: All participants must start at their designated flag-off times.
Course Compliance: Stay on the official route and follow guidance from race marshals and volunteers.
Littering: Strictly prohibited. Please dispose of waste at designated bins. Let's keep Patenga clean! 

Medical Support: Aid and hydration stations available throughout the course. Pets & Strollers: Not permitted on the racecourse for safety reasons.
 PRIZE MONEY RULES
Eligibility: Prize money will only be awarded if a category meets the minimum required number of participants.
Winners: Prizes awarded to top 3 male and female finishers in Full Marathon, Half Marathon & Challenge Run.
Presence Required: Winners must be present at the awards ceremony to claim prizes.
Verification: Identity and age verification may be required before awarding prizes.
 CANCELLATION & EVENT POLICY
Weather Policy: Rain or shine — the bay does not stop, and neither do we. Severe weather may delay the start but no refunds will be issued.
Transfer Policy: Registrations may be transferred to another runner within the given period (with applicable fees).
Refund Policy: No refunds under any circumstances, including injury, illness, or travel issues.
 CONDUCT & FAIR PLAY
Sportsmanship: Respect fellow runners, event staff, and volunteers at all times. Unsporting behavior may result in disqualification.
Complaints: Any dispute must be reported to race officials immediately after the race.
Spirit of Running: Run fair, run strong, and uphold the ActivePulse spirit — Fitness, Friendship & Fun!
