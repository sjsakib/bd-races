Why Join the AIMS Kids Run Series?
The AIMS KIDS RUN SERIES is a global initiative aimed at inspiring a love for running and fitness in young children around the world. In line with that goal, we organized a colorful and engaging running event exclusively for the kids in February 2025. The aim was to create an environment that promotes physical activity and well-being while bringing families closer. The spirit of the event was well understood by all little participants and their parents. The event became more than just a run— rather a celebration of health, happiness, and community.
With the success of season 1, RUNBANGLA has decided to reach out to the distant location of our country and community who do not have access to this type of big festival, designed for children. And SHERPUR RUNNERS COMMUNITY volunteered to become the partner of this global initiative. The main goal of the upcoming race is to ensure participation of 16 Indigenous communities living in the area those includes Garo, Koch, Hajong, Dalu, Horizon, Rajbongshi, Hodi, Banai, Barman, Mandai, Bedey. The kids are excited. As it is not possible for them to join any race outside the district, we are bringing the race to them.
Sherpur Runners Community, RUNBANGLA – AIMS (Association of International Marathons and Distance Races) is thrilled to announce the launch of the KIDS RUN SERIES in Sherpur, an exciting event dedicated to promoting health, fitness, and joy among young children in Bangladesh. This is the second event of its kind in the region, designed to bring together children aged 2 to 14 years for a memorable day of running, games, and community bonding!
Event Highlights:
Colorful Fun Run: Kids will run, play, and laugh through a safe 100 meters, 500 meters and 1KM course decorated with colorful stations, balloons, and cheering zones. It’s all about making memories, not speed!
