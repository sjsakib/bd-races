Together Against Breast Cancer
Pink Run is essentially a global social and athletic movement organized to raise Breast Cancer Awareness. The Pink Run is hosted in more than 30 countries worldwide to increase awareness, raise funds for cancer research and treatment, and honor survivors as well as those currently battling the disease.
For the past 12 years, alongside its other initiatives, Himu Paribahan has been conducting various activities for cancer awareness—including awareness runs, breast cancer screenings in colleges and universities, and distributing informational leaflets in various locations. In continuation of that tradition, Himu Paribahan is set to organize the 'Pink Run – 2026' at Hatirjheel this coming October 16.
