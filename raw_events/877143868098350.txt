Welcome to The Oldest AIMS race in Bangladesh!
After completion of 3 successful Half Marathons over last 2 years at the beautiful Jolshiri Abashon area, RUNBANGLA brings you a full Marathon at the same picturesque location. The beautiful Jolshiri Abashon area, Rupganj is a 30 minutes’ drive from Gulshan 2 circle, a housing society for military officers and managed by Bangladesh Army.
Your running track will consist of a well-connected road network with a view full of greens and lakes. This race would be a collaboration between Jolshiri Abashon authority and RUNBANGLA. In RUNBANGLA, we always strive to improve, reorganize and deliver better to the runners.

Official Rules and Regulations
1. Registration Terms and Eligibility
• Binding Agreement: Submission of the online registration form constitutes formal acknowledgement and legally binding acceptance of all Rules and Regulations.
• Entry Restrictions: Each unique email address permits only one registrant. Violation of this policy shall result in the withholding of race kits and immediate disqualification.
• Financial Policy: All registration fees are strictly non-refundable. Participant unique IDs and entries are non-transferable under any circumstances.
• Quota Authority: The Race Organizer reserves the absolute right to close registration prior to the deadline if event quotas are fulfilled.
• Data Integrity: Participants must provide true, accurate, and current information. Any age or qualification discrepancies found pre- or post-race will result in disqualification.
2. Bib Numbers and Timing Protocols
• Mandatory Display: The assigned bib number must be securely fastened to the front of the runner's torso and remain clearly visible at all times.
• Transfer Ban: Sale, duplication, or transfer of a race bib is strictly prohibited. Violation will result in immediate disqualification and a permanent ban from all future events.
• Timing and Checkpoints: Runners must wear the provided electronic timing chip. Missing any designated course checkpoints or failing to follow the official route will trigger automatic disqualification.
• Punctuality: Participants must start precisely at their designated official start times. Late starts are strictly prohibited.
3. Course Conduct and Restrictions
• Prohibited Behavior: Unsportsmanlike conduct, including but not limited to blocking or obstructing other participants, will result in immediate expulsion from the course.
• Strict Exclusions: No pets of any kind, unauthorized vehicles (including bicycles and motorcycles), or unregistered escort runners are permitted on the course.
• Official Compliance: Participants must strictly obey all instructions from race officials. Failure to comply will result in immediate disqualification.
• Attire: Participants must wear appropriate athletic attire. Clothing deemed offensive or inappropriate by the organizer is strictly forbidden.
4. Results, Awards, and Media Rights
• Time Limits: The official time calculation is the sole authority of the Race Organizer. Participants must fulfill the required Cut-Off Times.
• Medals and Certificates: Participation medals are strictly contingent upon crossing the entire official distance. Electronic certificates will only be issued to those finishing within the official cut-off time limits.
• Media Release: Participants grant the Race Organizer exclusive, worldwide rights to use their name, likeness, and biography across all media platforms for promotional purposes.
