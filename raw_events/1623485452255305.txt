Run. Rise. Win Over Diabetes.
Bangladesh, are you ready? The spirit of running is rising once again, the Pantonix RunRise Nation Diabetes Day Half Marathon 2026 is here, bigger, bolder, and more prestigious than ever!
This is a historic milestone for our community: RunRise Nation proudly presents its very first Half Marathon experience!
The iconic bridges of Hatirjheel and the rhythm of your heartbeat will together write a new chapter in history. This isn't just a race, it's a collective promise to step up for health, equity, and a lifestyle that beats the odds.
 Date: November 13, 2026 (Friday)

 Location: Hatirjheel, Dhaka

 Course Status: Officially an AIMS Certified and World Athletics Measure Certified course; ensuring an internationally standardized racing experience right here in Dhaka!
 TOGETHER, WE MOVE TOWARD THE GLOBAL GOALS
We are running with a deep purpose. This event is proudly aligned with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals to drive real impact:
* 
 SDG 3: Good Health & Well-Being – Promoting physical fitness, active lifestyles, and powerful diabetes awareness to safeguard our community's future.
* 
 SDG 10: Reduced Inequalities – Building an inclusive, accessible running space where everyone regardless of age, background, or athletic ability can line up at the start together.

 GENERAL RULES & GUIDELINES
Registration: Online only via our official website. No spot registration on race day.
