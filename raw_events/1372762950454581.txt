Albatross ULTRAIL 2026: The Evolution - 2nd Edition
Two Race Days. New Horizons. Ultimate Grit.
The Albatross ULTRAIL is returning to the untamed hills of Khagrachhari! After a historic debut, we are back with the 2nd Edition—and it’s bigger, tougher, and more inclusive than ever.
We’ve scouted deeper into the wild to bring you more technical tracks—think raw jungle single-tracks, sharper elevation spikes, and terrain that demands every ounce of your focus. Whether you are a seasoned ultra-runner or a trail newcomer, this edition is designed to push you further.
 Mayabini Lake ,Khagrachhari, Bangladesh

 [Insert Dates: 30 Oct To 1st Nov, 2026
Something New: A Two-Day Ultra Trail Race Festival
For the first time, the Albatross ULTRAIL will be held over two action-packed days. We’ve restructured the event to give more runners the chance to experience the magic of the hills:
 DAY 1: The Speed & Spirit Session The first day features our Moderate Distances. Perfect for those looking to test their speed on technical terrain or for runners making their debut in the world of trail running. Experience the thrill of the hills and the warmth of the trail community.
 DAY 2: The Ultimate Endurance Test The second day belongs to the Long Distances. This is where the legends are made. Designed for the gritty and the brave, these routes feature our newest, most challenging sections that will push your spirit to the limit.
★ What’s New for the 2nd Edition?

 Expanded Distances: We are adding more distance to the roster! From grueling new ultras to accessible challenges, there is a path for everyone. Distances to be announced soon!
 Technical Mastery: Expect a higher degree of technicality. More natural obstacles, hidden ravines, and authentic ridges that haven't been touched by city life.
 ITRA & UTMB Pathway: We continue our commitment to the global stage. This remains your premier gateway in Bangladesh to earn ITRA points and a UTMB Index.
 Greater Participation: By splitting the race over two days, we are opening the trails to a larger community of runners. Choose your day, or come for the whole weekend!
The Sky is Calling.
The Albatross is a bird of the open sea and endless sky—a symbol of the endurance required to travel thousands of miles. In 2026, we invite you to find that same spirit within yourself.
