BES Diabetes Day Run 2025

Organized by: Bangladesh Endocrine Society (BES)
Powered by: Vildamet

Join us for the Diabetes Day Run 2025 – a 7.5 km run to raise awareness and promote the importance of preventing and managing diabetes through physical activity.
 Venue: Hatirjheel Amphitheatre - হাতিরঝিল এম্ফি থিয়েটার, Dhaka

 Date: 14th November 2025 (Friday)

 Time: 6:00 AM sharp
