The world is heating up. Amidst the rush of our everyday lives, we often forget to pause and think about the planet we call home. But while we move forward, our Earth is slowly paying the price.
Global warming is changing our climate, intensifying natural disasters and making the future increasingly uncertain. The time to care is not tomorrow- it is now.
With a vision to raise awareness and inspire action for a greener planet, SUST Fitness Club is bringing you a half marathon that goes beyond running.
So, lace up your shoes, take the first step, and run with a purpose.
