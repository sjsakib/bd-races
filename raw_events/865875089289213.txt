For eight years, the BYLC Run has united thousands in a shared commitment to health, purpose, and community. In 2026, we continue this legacy with renewed ambition and an expanded challenge for youth and citizens across the nation.
This year’s 𝐁𝐘𝐋𝐂 𝐑𝐮𝐧𝐧𝐢𝐧𝐠 𝐰𝐢𝐭𝐡 𝐏𝐮𝐫𝐩𝐨𝐬𝐞 𝟐𝟎𝟐𝟔 offers participants even more ways to push their limits, featuring both the classic 7.5K route and a new 15K distance designed for those ready to take their fitness journey further. Our theme for 2026, “𝐇𝐞𝐚𝐥𝐭𝐡𝐲 𝐘𝐨𝐮𝐭𝐡, 𝐒𝐭𝐫𝐨𝐧𝐠𝐞𝐫 𝐍𝐚𝐭𝐢𝐨𝐧,” reflects our belief that the strength of Bangladesh lies in the well-being of its young people.
With a rapidly growing youth population, Bangladesh holds immense potential, but the rise of lifestyle-related health issues threatens to slow our national progress. By prioritizing youth health and encouraging active living, we can nurture a generation that is stronger, more resilient, and better prepared to lead. Aligned with Sustainable Development Goal (SDG) 3, "Good Health and Well-being," this year’s run emphasizes the powerful connection between healthier youth and a thriving nation.
Join us in this movement as we run toward a healthier you and a stronger Bangladesh!
Stay tuned for the registration date and more updates.
